How it works

From first text to final walkthrough.

  1. 1

    Send photos

    Text or email a few pictures and a sentence about what you need. No forms, no account.

  2. 2

    Get a firm price

    Usually within the hour. Flat rate for common jobs, written quote for anything bigger.

  3. 3

    Pick a slot

    Morning or afternoon window. You get a text when the tech is ten minutes out.

  4. 4

    Walk it with us

    We show you what was done, clean up, and send the invoice with a pay link that evening.
