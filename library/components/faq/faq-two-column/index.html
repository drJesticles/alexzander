Good to know

Quick answers.

The short version of what we get asked most. If yours is not here, call or text and a human answers.

What are your hours?
Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 5:30pm. Saturdays by appointment. Emergency calls answered seven days.
Is there a minimum charge?
One hour of labor, $95. If the job takes twenty minutes, use the rest of the hour on something else around the house.
How do I pay?
Card, check, cash or bank transfer when the job is done. Remodels are billed in three stages: deposit, midway, completion.
Do I need to be home?
Not for most work. Many customers leave a lockbox code or a garage code. We text photos when we arrive and when we leave.
Can you do work on a rental I own?
Yes. We manage turnover punch lists for a dozen local landlords and send itemized invoices you can hand straight to your accountant.
Do you pull permits?
When the city requires one, yes, and we include the fee in your quote. Most repairs and cosmetic updates do not need one.
What do you not do?
Roofing, HVAC replacement and electrical panels. We will give you the name of someone good for those.
Is the work guaranteed?
Labor is covered for one year. Materials carry the manufacturer's warranty, and we handle the claim for you.
