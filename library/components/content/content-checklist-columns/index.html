What we check

  • Water heater: age, anode rod, pressure valve
  • Every faucet, toilet and supply line for drips
  • GFCI outlets, smoke and CO detectors
  • Attic insulation depth and ventilation
  • Gutters, downspouts and grading at the foundation
  • Caulk and weatherstripping on doors and windows

What we do on the spot

  • Replace HVAC filters (bring your own or use ours)
  • Swap detector batteries
  • Tighten loose handles, hinges and towel bars
  • Flush the water heater
  • Re-caulk one tub or shower if needed
  • Leave a written report with photos and priorities
