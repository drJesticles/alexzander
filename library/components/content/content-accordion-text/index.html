The fine print, in plain English

Our policies, without the legalese.

Everything AAA Home Specialists promises, and what we ask of you in return. Expand any section.

Pricing and estimates

Repairs are priced flat-rate from a printed book, so the number does not change if a job runs long. Projects over $1,000 get a written proposal with each line itemized. Estimates are free; there is no charge for us to come look.

If we open a wall and find something we could not have seen, we stop, show you, and write a change order before continuing.

Scheduling and cancellations

You get a three-hour arrival window and a text when the tech is 30 minutes out. Cancel or move an appointment any time up to 5pm the day before at no charge. Same-day cancellations are billed one hour at the standard rate, which we waive for genuine emergencies.

Warranty

Workmanship is guaranteed for one year on repairs and two years on remodels. If something we installed fails because of how we installed it, we fix it free. Manufacturer warranties on fixtures and appliances are passed to you with the receipts.

The warranty does not cover damage from water intrusion, settling, or work done on top of ours by someone else.

Payment

Repairs are due on completion; the tech can take a card on the tablet, or you can pay the emailed invoice by bank transfer within seven days. Remodels are billed in three stages: 30 percent at signing, 40 percent at rough-in, 30 percent after your walk-through.
