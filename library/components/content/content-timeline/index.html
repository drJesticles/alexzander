Ten years in the River Valley

How AAA Home Specialists got here.

  1. 2016

    One truck, one phone

    Marcus Webb starts taking handyman calls out of a 2009 Silverado. First job: a storm door on Skyline Drive. The customer still calls every spring.

  2. 2019

    Second truck, first kitchen

    Danny Cole joins as lead carpenter. The two of them finish the company's first full kitchen remodel in eleven days, two under estimate.

  3. 2021

    The shop on West Main

    We sign the lease on 1214 West Main and build out a small showroom. Customers can finally touch a cabinet door before they buy it.

  4. 2023

    Four techs, every job on a tablet

    We move estimates, photos and invoices to a single app. Every customer gets same-day photos of finished work, a habit that started as a favor and became the rule.

  5. 2026

    500 homes and counting

    Our 500th repeat customer. Still two trucks on purpose, still the owner answering the phone before 8am.
