How we work

What to expect when you hire us for a remodel.

A remodel is a few weeks of strangers in your house. Here is exactly how AAA Home Specialists runs one, so there are no surprises on either side.

Before the first day

Every project starts with a written scope and a fixed price. We walk the space with you, measure twice, and send a proposal that lists each line of work with its cost. If you want to change something later, we write a change order before we touch it, never after. You will also get a start date and a realistic end date, with a weather buffer if any of the work is outside.

We pull the permits. Russellville requires them for structural, electrical and plumbing changes, and the inspections protect you as much as they protect us. Read more about which projects need permits on our journal.

During the work

  • Crews arrive between 7:30 and 8:00 and are off site by 5:00 unless we have agreed otherwise.
  • Floors, furniture and doorways are covered before any demo starts. We run a HEPA air scrubber during dusty phases.
  • You get a short text update every evening: what got done, what is next, and anything we need from you.
  • One lead tech owns the job from start to finish. You will know their name and their cell number.

The job is not finished when the last tile goes in. It is finished when you have walked through it with us and signed off.

How payment works

  1. A deposit of 30 percent at signing reserves your dates and covers special-order materials.
  2. A progress payment of 40 percent when rough-in inspections pass.
  3. The final 30 percent after your walk-through, once every punch-list item is closed.

We accept checks, cards and bank transfer. Financing through a local credit union is available on projects over $5,000; ask and we will send the link.

Workmanship on every remodel is covered for one full year. Manufacturer warranties on fixtures and appliances pass straight to you with the paperwork in your closing packet.
