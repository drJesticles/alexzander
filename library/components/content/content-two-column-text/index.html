Our story

Started with one truck and a promise to call people back.

AAA Home Specialists began in 2016 when Marcus Webb got tired of hearing neighbors in Russellville say the same thing: the contractor never showed, never called, never finished.

The first three years

Marcus worked alone out of a 2009 Silverado, doing the jobs nobody else wanted: a single door that would not latch, a cabinet hinge, a leaky hose bib. He answered every call himself, usually from a ladder. The rule was simple: say when you will be there, be there, and send the invoice the same day.

By 2019 there were more calls than hours. The second truck came with Danny Cole, a finish carpenter Marcus had worked beside on commercial jobs, and the two of them took on the first full kitchen.

Where we are now

Today it is four techs, two trucks and a small shop on West Main with a showroom for cabinet doors, tile and flooring samples. We still do the single-door jobs. They are how most people meet us, and the remodels tend to follow a year or two later.

What has not changed: the person who quotes your job does the work, every visit ends with photos in your inbox, and the phone gets answered. Danny still cuts every piece of crown molding himself.
