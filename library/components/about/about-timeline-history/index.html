Our history

Twenty-two years in the River Valley.

  1. 2004

    One truck, one phone

    Dale leaves a framing crew and starts AAA Home Specialists out of his garage on South Arkansas Avenue. First job: a storm door in Dardanelle.

  2. 2009

    First hire, first shop

    Luis joins as a carpenter and the company moves into a rented bay off Highway 7 with room for a lumber rack and a second truck.

  3. 2015

    Landlord service starts

    A property manager asks for monthly turnover help on twelve units. That program now covers more than a hundred rentals across Pope and Yell counties.

  4. 2020

    Smart home division

    Evan comes on to handle thermostats, cameras and networking as half of all calls start including something with an app.

  5. 2026

    Six on the crew, 1,400 jobs

    Same owner, same promise. Most new work still comes from a neighbor who saw the truck in a driveway.
