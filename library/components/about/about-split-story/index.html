About us

A handyman company that runs like a real business.

AAA Home Specialists started in 2004 with one truck and one promise: do the small jobs the big contractors will not touch, and do them properly. Two decades on, we are still based in Russellville, still answering our own phone, and still finishing most jobs the same day we start them.

  • Licensed and insured. Arkansas contractor license, $1M liability, workers' comp on every tech.
  • Priced before we start. Flat rates on common jobs, written quotes on the rest, no surprise line items.
  • Guaranteed for a year. If something we fixed fails, we come back and make it right, free.
