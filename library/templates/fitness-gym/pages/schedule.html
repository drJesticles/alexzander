Class policies

The rules, in plain English.

How booking, waitlists and late arrivals work at Iron Valley Fitness. Expand any section.

Booking and waitlists

Book in the app or at the desk. Plus members see 7 days out, Unlimited 10. Full classes have a waitlist; if a spot opens you get a text and 30 minutes to confirm before it goes to the next person.

Cancellations and no-shows

Cancel up to 2 hours before class for free. Late cancels and no-shows count as a used class on Plus; three no-shows in a month on Unlimited pauses advance booking for a week. We are strict because twelve spots fill and someone on the waitlist wanted yours.

Late arrivals

Five minutes late is fine, join the warm-up. Past ten minutes the coach may ask you to do open gym instead, for your safety and the class's pace. Strength classes start loading bars at the top of the hour.

Holidays and weather

Badge access stays on 24/7 every day of the year, including Christmas. Classes run a holiday schedule on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and New Year's Day, posted in the app a week ahead. Ice storms cancel classes but never the badge.

Free 7-day pass

Drop your email and we send a pass good for a week of classes, open gym and one session with a trainer.
