Where do I start?

Three starting points, depending on where you are.

Start with Foundations. Six weeks, twice a week.

You will learn to squat, hinge, press and row with a coach watching every rep, in a group of people who are also new. By week six you can walk into any class on the schedule and know what to do.

Join the next Foundations group
  • New groups start the first Monday of every month
  • Tuesday and Thursday at 6am, noon, or 6pm
  • Included in Plus and Unlimited memberships
  • HIIT, Spin and Mobility are open to you from day one
  • Free 30-minute trainer session to set your starting weights

Took a few years off? Ease in without the ego.

Book the free trainer session first. If your lifts are still solid, go straight into Strength at reduced loads. If they are rusty, two weeks of Foundations will get them back faster than guessing on your own.

Book the free assessment
  • Free movement assessment with any membership
  • Coach sets conservative starting loads for your first block
  • Mobility class twice a week to undo the desk years
  • HIIT scaled to bike and rower until the joints are ready
  • Progress logged in the app so you can see it coming back

Already lifting? Skip Foundations and get coached.

Show a coach your main lifts in the free assessment and you are cleared for Strength the same day. Open gym has eight racks, two platforms, calibrated plates and chalk, and it is never full at 5am.

Compare memberships
  • Same-day clearance for Strength after a coach check
  • Eight power racks, two deadlift platforms, competition bars
  • Meet prep and programming from Coach Marcus ($120 a month)
  • Quarterly in-house powerlifting meet, members only
  • Open gym from 5am; racks are rarely all taken before 4pm
