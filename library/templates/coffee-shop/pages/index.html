Six things Bluebird Coffee Roasters gets right every morning, whether you order a black drip or a lavender oat latte.

Roasted this week

Every bag on the shelf has a roast date, and nothing older than ten days goes in the hopper.

Open at 6:30, six days

Early enough for the Tech campus and the first shift at the plant. Drive-up window on the alley side.

Direct-trade farms

We buy from four farms we have visited, in Huila, Sidama, Chiapas and Nariño, and pay above Fair Trade minimums.

Baked in the back

Cinnamon rolls, scones and quiche come out of our oven at 6am. When the case is empty, it is empty.

Baristas who remember your order

Most of the crew has been here three years or more. Say your name twice and it is on the cup forever.

Every tenth drink free

No app required. Give us your phone number at the register and the punches track themselves.
