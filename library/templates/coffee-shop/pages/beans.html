Our beans

Four farms we have stood on. One roaster we know by sound.

Bluebird Coffee Roasters buys green coffee directly from growers in Huila and Nariño in Colombia, Sidama in Ethiopia, and Chiapas in Mexico. Owner Jess Cordell visits two of them every year and pays a fixed price agreed before harvest, not whatever the market says in May.

Every batch goes through the 12-kilo Diedrich in the back room on Tuesdays and Fridays. Roast dates are stamped on the bag, and anything older than ten days gets pulled from the bar.

  • Direct trade with four farms, visited in person
  • Small batches, twice a week, roast date on every bag
  • Cupped and scored before it ever reaches the hopper

Farm to cup

How a bean gets to your mug.

  1. 1

    Buy at the farm

    We agree a price with the grower before harvest and buy the whole lot, so they can plan and so can we.

  2. 2

    Sample roast and cup

    Every arriving lot gets roasted three ways and scored blind by the crew. Below 84 points, it does not go on the bar.

  3. 3

    Roast in small batches

    Twelve kilos at a time, Tuesdays and Fridays. We log every batch and taste the first bag out of each one.

  4. 4

    Rest, then serve

    Beans rest three days before they hit the grinder and never serve past ten. Bags you buy are stamped with the date.

Our story

Nine years on Commerce Avenue.

  1. 2017

    A cart at the farmers market

    Jess Cordell roasts on a 1-kilo drum in her garage in Atkins and sells pour-overs from a cart at the Russellville farmers market on Saturdays.

  2. 2018

    The shop opens

    Bluebird Coffee Roasters signs the lease on the old pharmacy at 118 North Commerce. The soda fountain counter becomes the espresso bar.

  3. 2020

    The alley window

    We cut a drive-up window into the alley wall in April. It was supposed to be temporary. It is now a third of our mornings.

  4. 2022

    First trip to Huila

    Jess and head roaster Caleb visit the Suárez family farm and sign the first direct contract. The Huila has been on the bar ever since.

  5. 2026

    Twelve staff, four farms, twenty wholesale accounts

    Still one shop on purpose. The cinnamon roll recipe has not changed since the farmers market cart.
