How we work

What hiring Whitaker & Lane actually looks like.

Most people have never hired a lawyer and do not know what to expect. Here is the honest version, from the first phone call to the last invoice.

The first meeting

The consultation is free and lasts about 45 minutes. You meet the attorney who would handle your case, not a paralegal or an intake specialist. Bring whatever paperwork you have: the police report, the summons, the insurance letter, the old will. We will tell you whether you have a case, what it is likely worth or likely to cost, and what we would do first.

If we are not the right fit, we say so and give you a name. About one in four consultations ends that way, and we would rather lose the file than take a case we cannot do well.

After you hire us

You get a written fee agreement in plain English, the attorney's direct cell number, and a client portal where every filing and letter shows up the day it is sent. Calls are returned the same business day. Hourly matters are billed monthly in six-minute increments with a line for every entry; you will never see a mystery charge.

Injury cases cost you nothing up front. We advance the costs, and if there is no recovery, you owe us nothing. That is not a slogan; it is in the agreement.
