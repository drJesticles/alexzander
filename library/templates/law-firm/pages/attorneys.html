How we practice

Rules we set in 2006 and have not needed to change.

Whitaker & Lane was started by two lawyers who were tired of big-firm billing and small-firm disorganization. These four habits are the result.

  • Calls returned the same day

    You get the attorney's cell number at the first meeting. If we are in court, a paralegal tells you when we will be out.

  • Fees in writing, first

    Flat fees where we can, hourly estimates where we cannot, and never a bill you did not see coming.

  • Honest odds

    If your case is weak, we say so at the consultation. We would rather you hear it from us for free than from a judge for a fee.

  • Local, on purpose

    We practice in three counties and know every courtroom in them. We do not take cases in Little Rock and we do not advertise on billboards.
