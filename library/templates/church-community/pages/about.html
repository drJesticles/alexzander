What we believe

The short version of a long tradition.

Riverside Community Church is a non-denominational church that holds to the historic Christian faith as summarized in the Apostles' and Nicene Creeds. Here is what that means on a Sunday.

  • The Bible is true

    We believe Scripture is God's word, trustworthy and sufficient. It is why we preach through it book by book instead of picking topics.

  • Jesus is the center

    Fully God, fully man, crucified and risen. Salvation is a gift received by faith, not earned by being good enough, which is a relief for all of us.

  • The church is a family

    Not a building or a Sunday event. We practice baptism and communion, we meet in homes during the week, and we carry each other's burdens.

  • We are sent to the town

    Russellville is not our audience; it is our neighbor. We give away a fifth of our budget and expect every member to serve somewhere.

Our story

Twenty-three years on the river.

  1. 2003

    Eleven people in a living room

    Dan and Carol Hargrove open their home on Skyline Drive on Sunday nights. Bible study, a potluck, and a lot of people who had given up on church.

  2. 2006

    The middle school cafeteria

    Sixty people and a trailer full of chairs. Setup at 7am every Sunday for three years. Some of those setup crew members still run the lobby.

  3. 2009

    The old gym on Knoxville

    Riverside Community Church buys a closed-down recreation center. The basketball lines are still under the carpet in the sanctuary.

  4. 2017

    The food pantry opens

    A Tuesday-morning pantry in the old locker rooms. It now serves 180 families a month from across Pope and Yell counties.

  5. 2026

    Two services, thirty groups

    Around 400 people most Sundays and a second service added in spring. Still a potluck on the first Sunday of every month.

Leadership

The pastors and staff.

Three pastors, a handful of staff, and a board of seven elders elected by the congregation. All of them are reachable; most of them are in the lobby on Sunday.

Dan Hargrove

Lead pastor

Started the church in his living room in 2003. Preaches most Sundays, drinks too much coffee, and still visits every hospital room.

Maria Santos

Worship and arts

Leads the band and the choir that shows up at Christmas and Easter. Arkansas Tech music grad, grew up in Dardanelle.

Tyler Boone

Students and families

Runs Wednesday nights for 6th through 12th grade and the summer camp trip. Knows every kid's name by their second visit.

Joyce Pruitt

Care and food pantry

Runs the Tuesday pantry, the meal train and the benevolence fund. If you need help and do not know who to call, call Joyce.
