Calendar

Coming up at Riverside Community Church.

  1. Oct 25

    Fall Festival

    Saturday 4pm to 7pm on the front lawn. Hayrides, a chili cook-off judged by the fire department, bounce houses and trunk-or-treat. Free and open to the whole town.

  2. Nov 2

    Newcomers lunch

    After the 11:00 service in the fellowship hall. Lunch with the pastors, the story of the church, and a chance to ask anything. Childcare provided.

  3. Nov 22

    Thanksgiving box packing

    Saturday 9am to 11am. We pack 250 full Thanksgiving dinners for families referred by the schools and the pantry. All ages welcome; pizza afterward.

  4. Dec 7

    Marriage class begins

    Six Sunday evenings, 5pm to 6:30pm, for couples at any stage. Dinner and childcare included. Register by November 30; limited to 20 couples.

  5. Dec 24

    Christmas Eve services

    4pm and 6pm. Carols, candles, the Christmas story, about an hour. The 4pm is the family one; the 6pm is quieter. Invite someone who has not been in a while.
