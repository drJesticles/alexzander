Ministries

Where to plug in, whatever stage of life you are in.

Sunday is the front door. These are the rooms behind it. Every one is open to anyone, member or not.

Small groups

Thirty groups of 8 to 14 people meeting in homes across Russellville, Dardanelle, Atkins and Dover. Dinner, a Bible study, and people who notice when you are not there.

Find a group

Riverside Kids

Nursery through 5th grade during both Sunday services. Safe check-in, background-checked teachers, and a curriculum that walks through the whole Bible in three years.

What to expect for kids

Students

6th through 12th grade, Wednesdays 6:30 to 8pm in the old gym. Worship, teaching, small groups by grade, and a summer camp trip to Petit Jean every June.

Wednesday nights

Food pantry

Tuesdays 9am to noon, no paperwork, no questions beyond your name. 180 families a month. We always need drivers and shelf stockers.

Volunteer or get help

Care and counseling

Grief groups, a marriage class twice a year, hospital visits, a meal train for new babies and surgeries, and referrals to licensed counselors we trust.

Ask for help

Missions

Long-term partners in Guatemala and East Africa, two trips a year, and support for three church plants in the River Valley. A fifth of our budget goes out the door.
