How a repair works here

Three steps, and you approve every dollar in between.

  1. We inspect and photograph

    A technician looks at the whole car, not just the complaint, and photographs anything worn. You get the pictures by text.

  3. You get a written estimate

    Parts, labor and tax, sorted into "fix now," "fix soon" and "watch." Nothing is done until you tap approve.

  5. We fix it and warranty it

    Most jobs done the same day. Old parts in a box if you want them. 24 months or 24,000 miles on parts and labor.
