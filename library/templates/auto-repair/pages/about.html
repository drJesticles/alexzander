From the owner

A note before you hand us the keys.

Russellville, Arkansas

Hi neighbor,

My dad opened this shop in 1998 with two bays and a tire machine he bought at auction. I grew up sweeping the floor, and I have run the place since 2015. Most of what I know about fixing cars I learned here. Most of what I know about running a business I learned from watching him refuse to sell people things they did not need.

That is still the whole philosophy at Main Street Auto & Tire. We photograph the worn part, we write the number down before we start, and we tell you when something can wait. Six ASE-certified technicians, three of them here more than ten years. Half of our customers have been coming since my dad's time.

If we ever fall short of that, call me directly at (479) 555-0150 and ask for Luke. I would rather hear it from you than read it online.

Luke Hendricks

Luke HendricksOwner, Main Street Auto & Tire
