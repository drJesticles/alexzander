Billing questions

The small print, short.

Everything about trials, billing and switching plans. Anything else, email hello@fieldnote.app.

Is the trial really free?
Yes. Fourteen days of the Crew plan, no card required. When it ends you pick a plan or the account pauses; nothing is charged automatically.
What counts as a user?
Anyone who signs in: office staff, techs, the owner. Customers approving quotes and paying invoices are not users and are always free.
Can I switch plans later?
Any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.
Are there setup or cancellation fees?
No. Data import is included on Crew and Fleet. Cancel from the billing page and export everything as CSV for 90 days afterwards.
Is card processing extra?
Processing is 2.9 percent plus 30 cents per card payment and 1 percent for bank transfers, capped at $10. There is no monthly processing fee.
Do you offer discounts?
Yearly billing saves two months on every plan. Nonprofits and trade schools get 30 percent off; email us with your details.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, American Express and ACH for yearly plans. Invoicing on net-30 terms is available for Fleet.
Where is my data stored?
In US data centres, encrypted at rest and in transit, backed up every hour. A full security overview is in the documentation.
