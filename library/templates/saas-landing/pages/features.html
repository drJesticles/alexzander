Features

Schedule it. Quote it. Get paid for it.

A dispatch board the office actually likes

Drag a job onto a tech and a time. Fieldnote checks drive time, warns about overlaps, and texts the customer their window. Recurring jobs schedule themselves.

  • Day, week and map views
  • Route optimisation across the whole crew
  • Automatic "on my way" texts with a live link
Try scheduling free

Quotes that close from the driveway

Build a quote on the phone from your price book, add photos, offer good-better-best options, and send it before you leave. The customer approves with one tap and can pay a deposit on the spot.

  • Price book with bundles and markups
  • Optional line items and upsells
  • Automatic follow-ups on unanswered quotes
Send your first quote

Invoices that get paid in days, not months

Turn a finished job into an invoice in one tap. Customers pay by card, bank transfer or financing from the text. Reminders go out on your schedule, and everything lands in QuickBooks or Xero.

  • Card and bank payments, next-day payouts
  • Deposits, progress billing and financing
  • Two-way accounting sync
