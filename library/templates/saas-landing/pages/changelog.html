Changelog

What shipped, and when.

Every release, in plain language. Fieldnote ships most weeks; the big ones are listed here.

Version 3.2

Live "on my way" texts with a map link

When a tech taps Start Driving, the customer gets a text with a live map and an ETA that updates until arrival. Off by default; turn it on per crew in Settings.

Oct 9, 2026 · Scheduling

Version 3.1

Good, better, best quote options

Offer up to three options on one quote. The customer picks one, approves and pays a deposit in a single screen. Early users report a 22 percent higher average ticket.

Sep 18, 2026 · Quotes

Version 3.0

Crew app rebuilt with full offline mode

The day's jobs, photos, checklists and signatures all work with no signal and sync when a bar returns. Also: dark mode, and the app opens twice as fast.

Aug 5, 2026 · Mobile

Version 2.9

Two-way Xero sync, plus QuickBooks class tracking

Xero joins QuickBooks as a supported ledger. QuickBooks users can now map Fieldnote locations and crews to classes for cleaner P&L reports.

Jun 24, 2026 · Accounting
