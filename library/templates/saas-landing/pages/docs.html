Getting started with Fieldnote.

From a blank account to your first paid invoice in about an hour. This page covers the four steps; every other guide in the Fieldnote docs assumes you have done them.

1. Import your customers

Go to Customers, then Import. Fieldnote accepts a CSV with any column names; you map them on the next screen. Exports from Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan and QuickBooks are recognised automatically. Duplicates are matched on phone number and merged, not doubled.

On the Crew and Fleet plans you can skip this step and email the file to hello@fieldnote.app; we import it for you within one business day.

2. Build your price book

  • Add services with a name, a default duration and a price. Prices can be flat, hourly or per unit.
  • Group services into bundles for common jobs, such as "Water heater replacement, 40 gal".
  • Set a materials markup percentage once; it applies to every parts line unless you override it.
  • Mark optional items as upsells so they appear as add-ons on quotes.

3. Invite the crew

Settings, then Team, then Invite. Each person gets a text with a link to the iOS or Android app. Techs see their own jobs; office roles see the dispatch board. On Fleet, fine-grained permissions are available per role.

Most teams find it easiest to run one day on paper and Fieldnote side by side, then switch completely the next morning.

4. Connect payments

  1. Settings, then Payments, then Connect. You will need your business bank account and EIN.
  2. Approval usually takes a few minutes; occasionally a day if the bank asks for documents.
  3. Send a $1 test invoice to yourself and pay it from the text to see the whole loop.

That is it. Your online booking page is live at your Fieldnote subdomain from the moment the account exists; add the link to your website and Google Business Profile whenever you are ready.

Stuck on any step? Chat is in the bottom-right corner of the app, weekdays 7am to 7pm Central. Median first response is under four minutes.

Scheduling and dispatch

The dispatch board shows every job by tech and time. Drag to move, stretch to change duration, right-click to reassign. Recurring jobs are created from the customer record and appear on the board automatically. Route optimisation runs per tech per day; press Optimise to reorder the day by drive time.

Customer notifications are configured under Settings, then Messaging. Each template supports variables such as the tech's name and the arrival window.

Quotes and approvals

Quotes are built from the price book and sent by text or email with a link. Customers approve with one tap, which can require a deposit. Unanswered quotes trigger follow-ups on the schedule you set, up to three. Approved quotes convert to jobs with one click and carry every line item through to the invoice.

Invoices, payments and accounting

Completing a job creates a draft invoice. Send it by text; the customer pays by card, bank transfer or financing. Payouts arrive the next business day. QuickBooks and Xero sync runs every fifteen minutes and maps Fieldnote services to your income accounts; the mapping is under Settings, then Accounting.

Refunds and partial payments are handled from the invoice page and sync back to the ledger as credit notes.

API and webhooks (Fleet)

The REST API covers customers, jobs, quotes, invoices and payments. Authenticate with a bearer token created under Settings, then Developers. Webhooks fire on job status changes, quote approvals and payments, with signed payloads and automatic retries for 24 hours. Rate limit is 600 requests per minute per account.
