Founder

Dr. Nisha Patel opened the practice with two chairs and a promise not to upsell.

After residency in Little Rock and four years in a corporate chain where every visit came with a sales quota, Dr. Patel moved home to the River Valley and opened River Valley Family Dental in 2008 with one rule: recommend only what she would do for her own family.

Dr. Mark Owens joined in 2016 to handle surgery, root canals and implants in-house. Together with eleven staff they now see about 3,000 patients a year from Russellville, Dardanelle, Atkins and Hector.
