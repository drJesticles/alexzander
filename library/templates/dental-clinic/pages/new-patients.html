New patients

Your first visit, start to finish.

A new patient visit at River Valley Family Dental takes about 75 minutes. Without insurance it is a flat $149 for the exam, full X-rays and a cleaning.

Fill out the form below

What to bring

  • Photo ID and your insurance card, if you have one
  • A list of medications, including vitamins
  • Your previous dentist's name so we can request X-rays
  • Any night guard, retainer or partial you currently wear
  • Questions. Write them down; we will go through all of them
  • For kids under 18, a parent or signed consent form

What happens

  • Five-minute check-in if forms are done online
  • Full-mouth digital X-rays and photos
  • Gum measurements and oral cancer screening
  • A regular cleaning, unless deep cleaning is needed
  • The dentist reviews everything with you on screen
  • A written plan and insurance estimate before you leave

If you are in pain, skip the new patient visit and call (479) 555-0121 for a same-day emergency exam instead. We will do the full visit later.
