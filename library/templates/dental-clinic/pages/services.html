What a visit looks like

From the front door to the treatment plan.

  1. 1

    Check in

    Forms done online ahead of time, so you are in a chair within five minutes of your appointment.

  2. 2

    X-rays and photos

    Digital X-rays with a fraction of the old radiation, plus intraoral photos you can see on the screen.

  3. 3

    Clean and exam

    Hygienist first, then the dentist walks through every tooth with you, pointing at the pictures.

  4. 4

    Plan and price

    If anything needs doing, you leave with a written plan, the insurance estimate, and no pressure to book today.
