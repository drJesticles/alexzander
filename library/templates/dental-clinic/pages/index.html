How we practice

No surprise bills. No lectures. No drilling you did not agree to.

Dr. Patel and Dr. Owens built River Valley Family Dental around the four things patients told them they hated about going to the dentist.

  • The price before the chair

    A written estimate with your insurance applied, handed to you before we schedule anything beyond the exam.

  • Your X-rays on the screen

    You see what we see. If we recommend a filling, you will see the shadow we are pointing at and why it will not fix itself.

  • Nervous patients welcome

    Headphones, blankets, breaks whenever you raise a hand, and nitrous or oral sedation for the visits that need it.

  • On time, or we tell you

    We schedule real appointment lengths. If we are running more than ten minutes behind, the front desk texts you before you leave home.
