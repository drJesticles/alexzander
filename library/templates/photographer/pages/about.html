About Hollis

Eleven years, three hundred weddings, one camera bag.

Hollis Reed Photography started in 2015 with a borrowed camera and a friend's wedding in a backyard in Fayetteville. The approach has not changed: arrive early, stay quiet, and photograph what is actually happening instead of directing it. Hollis still edits every image personally and still answers the phone.

  • Documentary first. A short list of family portraits, then the rest of the day unfolds on its own.
  • Film and digital. Two cameras, backed up twice before leaving the venue.
  • Insured and licensed. Liability cover every venue in Northwest Arkansas has already seen.
