From the owner

A note before you hire us.

Russellville, Arkansas

Hi neighbor,

Thanks for looking us up. I know a website is a strange place to decide who gets the gate code, so here is the short version of who we are.

I started Green Acre Lawn & Landscape in 2013 with one mower and a trailer because I kept hearing the same thing from neighbors: the lawn guy showed up whenever, scalped the yard, and disappeared in August. I thought a yard deserved the same crew every week, cut at the right height, by people who know what Bermuda wants in July.

Thirteen years and four crews later that is still the whole business plan. If we ever fall short of it, call me directly at (479) 555-0184. I would rather hear it from you than read it online.

Our mission

Be the crew the whole street ends up hiring.

That means showing up the same day every week, cutting the grass the way the grass wants, and planting things that will still be alive in three summers. Everything Green Acre Lawn & Landscape does comes back to four habits.

  • Same day, same crew

    Your yard is on a route with a name on it. A text the night before, and a call the moment rain moves anything.

  • Quote it straight

    The number we give is the number on the invoice. If we find something extra, we stop and ask first.

  • Plant for Arkansas

    Clay soil, 100-degree Augusts and ice storms. We only plant what survives here, and we guarantee it for a year.

  • Leave it cleaner

    Clippings blown off, gates latched, debris hauled. The only evidence we were there is the stripes.
