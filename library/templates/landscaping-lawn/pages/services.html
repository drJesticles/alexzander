How it works

From first call to first mow.

  1. 1

    Send your address

    Text or call with the address. We measure the lot from a satellite view and ask about gates and dogs.

  2. 2

    Get a per-visit price

    Usually within the hour. Flat rate per mow, written quote for landscape and hardscape work.

  3. 3

    Pick your day

    Same day every week, same crew. You get a text the evening before and when the crew is on the way.

  4. 4

    Never think about it again

    Card on file, billed monthly, photos after each visit. Skip a week or pause for vacation by text.
