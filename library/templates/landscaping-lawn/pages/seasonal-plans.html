Season by season

What your yard needs, and when.

March to May: wake it up and get ahead of the weeds.

Pre-emergent goes down before soil hits 55 degrees or crabgrass wins the year. Beds get cleaned, mulched and edged, and mowing starts the week the Bermuda greens up.

Book a spring cleanup
  • Pre-emergent and first feeding
  • Spring cleanup: leaves, cutbacks, bed edging
  • Fresh mulch or pine straw
  • Irrigation start-up and head check
  • Weekly mowing begins

June to August: keep it green through the heat.

Weekly mowing at the right height for the grass type, grub control before the damage shows, and shrubs shaped after they bloom. We watch for fungus and armyworms so you do not have to.

Start weekly mowing
  • Weekly mow, trim, edge and blow
  • Summer feeding and grub control
  • Shrub trimming after bloom
  • Irrigation adjustments for the heat
  • Fungus and armyworm treatment as needed

September to February: set up next year.

Fall is when lawns are made. Green Acre Lawn & Landscape aerates and overseeds fescue, winterizes everything, and clears the leaves in two or three passes so they never smother the grass. Winter is for patios and walls.

Book a fall cleanup
  • Aeration and overseeding (fescue)
  • Fall winterizer feeding
  • Leaf removal, two to three visits
  • Irrigation blow-out before the first freeze
  • Hardscape and planting season
