What the week looks like.

Every exterior gets a day-by-day plan before we start, so you know when to move the cars and when the windows can open again.

  1. Day 0

    Walkaround and color check

    We confirm colors with sample boards on the actual siding in sun and shade, photograph everything, and flag any rotten wood.

  2. Day 1

    Pressure wash

    Whole house washed to strip chalk, mildew and loose paint. Then it dries for a day; paint on damp wood is how peeling starts.

  3. Day 2–3

    Scrape, repair, caulk, prime

    Every loose edge scraped and sanded, rotten trim replaced, every seam caulked, bare wood primed. The slow part, and the part that matters.

  4. Day 4

    Two coats

    Siding sprayed and back-rolled, trim and doors brushed. Windows, plants and cars stay masked until the second coat is dry.

  5. Day 5

    Walkaround and handoff

    You walk it with the lead painter in daylight, we touch up anything on the list, pull the masking and leave labeled leftover paint.

Pricing and what is included

Exteriors are quoted per house after a walkaround, with the wash, prep, primer, two coats of paint and cleanup on one written price. A typical 1,800 sq ft single-story starts around $3,500; two-story homes start around $5,500. Estimates are free.

Rotten trim or siding is priced per board when we find it. If we find more under the old paint, we stop, show you, and write a change order before replacing it.

Weather and timing

We paint exteriors from March through November, above 50 degrees and below 90 on the surface, with no rain in the forecast for 24 hours. If the weather turns, we stop and come back; paint applied in the wrong conditions is the number one cause of early peeling. Your start date is set in writing and we call the day before.

Warranty

Exterior work is guaranteed for five years against peeling, flaking and blistering. If it fails because of our prep or application, we scrape, prime and repaint the area free. Manufacturer warranties on the paint itself are passed to you with the receipts.

The warranty does not cover damage from water intrusion behind the siding, settling, hail, or surfaces that were not part of the job.

Products we use

Siding gets a 100 percent acrylic exterior paint, usually Sherwin-Williams Duration or Benjamin Moore Aura, which stretches and shrinks with the siding through an Arkansas summer. Bare wood gets an oil or bonding primer first. Decks and fences get a penetrating stain, not a film, so they do not peel. We list the exact product and sheen on your quote.
