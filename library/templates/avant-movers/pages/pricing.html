Good to know

Quick answers about the bill.

The short version of what we get asked most. If yours is not here, call or text and a human answers.

What are your hours?
Monday to Saturday, 7am to 7pm. Sunday moves by appointment. The office answers seven days during moving season.
Is there a minimum?
Two hours on local moves and labor-only jobs. A studio or a single room usually lands right at the minimum.
How do I pay?
Local moves are paid at the end, card or check, with the crew lead. Long-distance moves take a 20 percent deposit to hold the date and the balance on delivery.
Do I need to be there?
Someone needs to let us in and point at rooms at both ends. For long-distance, you or someone you trust signs the inventory at load and at delivery.
What is a not-to-exceed price?
The most you will pay on an hourly move, in writing, based on the rooms and photos you gave us. If the crew finishes early you pay less; if it runs long, you do not pay more.
Are stairs and elevators extra?
They are part of the quote, not an add-on. Tell us about them up front and the number already includes them. Nothing gets added on moving day.
What do you not move?
Propane tanks, paint, ammunition, plants on long-distance trips, and anything alive. We will tell you how to handle those.
Do you tip the crew?
Never required, always appreciated. Most customers who tip do $20 to $40 per mover for a local move. Cold drinks on a July afternoon count too.
