Three kinds of moves

Across town, across the state, or across the country.

Local: hourly, with a ceiling

Clock starts when the truck arrives and stops when the last box is in the room you pointed to. You get a not-to-exceed number in writing, and most moves come in under it. Two-hour minimum, no travel fee in the regular area.

  • Studio or 1-bed: two movers, about 3 hours
  • 2 or 3-bed: three movers, 4 to 6 hours
  • 4-bed and up: four movers and two trucks

Long-distance: one flat price, one crew

A 20-minute video walkthrough gets you a binding price, not an estimate. Your belongings ride alone on a dedicated truck with the same crew that loaded them, and deliver on the day we agreed, not a two-week window.

  • Binding quote after a video walkthrough
  • Dedicated truck, no shared loads
  • Next-day delivery within 500 miles

Packing: the day before, done by people who do it daily

A packing crew comes the day before the move with boxes, paper, tape and dish barrels. Every box is labeled by room and contents. Unpacking at the other end puts things in cabinets and takes the empty boxes away.

  • Full-house or kitchen-and-breakables only
  • All materials included, wardrobe boxes on loan
  • Unpacking and box removal available
