Six things Avant Movers does on every move, from a studio across town to a four-bedroom to Dallas.

An arrival time, not a window

Your crew is scheduled for 8am or 1pm and texts you when the truck leaves the yard. First move of the day is never late.

Written quotes, binding on long-distance

Local moves are hourly with a not-to-exceed number. Long-distance is a flat, binding price after a video walkthrough. No weight tickets, no surprises.

Licensed and insured

USDOT and Arkansas intrastate authority, cargo and liability coverage, workers' comp on every mover. Ask for the certificate; we'll email it.

Our movers, not day labor

Every mover is a trained, background-checked employee. The same crew loads and unloads, and the lead has done at least 200 moves.

We protect both houses

Floor runners, door-jamb pads and rail covers go on before the first box moves. Your deposit and your new floors stay intact.

Full-value protection available

Basic coverage is included. Full replacement-value protection is available on every move for a flat fee, and claims are settled in 14 days.
