Moving checklist

The list we hand every customer. Print it.

Most moving-day stress is a thing that should have happened three weeks ago. Here is what to do, and when, so the day itself is just carrying.

Get a quote first

Four to eight weeks out

  • Book the movers and get the date in writing
  • Purge: sell, donate or dump anything you would not pay to move
  • Order boxes, or book packing, and start with the garage and closets
  • Transfer utilities, internet and mail for the move date
  • Reserve the elevator or loading zone if either building needs it
  • Gather records: school, medical, vet, and the car title

Move week

  • Defrost the freezer and use up the fridge
  • Pack a first-night box: sheets, towels, chargers, coffee, meds
  • Set aside what rides with you: documents, jewelry, cash, keys
  • Label every box by room, and tape a room map by the new front door
  • Clear a path from every room to the door; pets and kids somewhere safe
  • Walk the empty house with the crew lead before the truck leaves

Booked with us? Your coordinator texts these reminders on schedule, so you do not have to remember the list.

Moving day

How a Avant Movers moving day runs.

  1. 7:30

    Truck leaves the yard

    You get a text with the crew lead's name and a 30-minute heads-up. First move of the day, so no earlier job can run late into yours.

  2. 8:00

    Walkthrough and protection

    The lead walks the house with you, confirms what goes and what stays, then runners, jamb pads and rail covers go on before anything moves.

  3. 8:15

    Load

    Boxes first, then wrapped furniture, heaviest pieces low and forward. Beds come apart last so the bedroom is the last room touched. A two-bedroom loads in about two hours.

  4. 10:30

    Drive and unload

    You walk the empty house with the lead, then meet us at the new one. Beds go up first so the day ends with somewhere to sleep, then every box to the room on its label.

  5. 12:30

    Walkthrough and sign-off

    Appliances reconnected, furniture where you pointed, pads and runners off, empty boxes stacked. You walk it with the lead, sign the inventory, and the clock stops.
