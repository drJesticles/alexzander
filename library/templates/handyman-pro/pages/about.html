About us

A handyman company that answers the phone.

River Valley Handyman Co. started in 2011 with one truck and one promise: do the small jobs the big contractors will not touch, and do them properly. Fifteen years on, we are still based in Russellville, still answering our own phone, and still finishing most jobs the same day we start them.

  • Licensed and insured. Arkansas contractor license, $1M liability, workers' comp on every tech.
  • Priced before we start. Flat rates on common jobs, written quotes on the rest, no surprise line items.
  • Guaranteed for a year. If something we fixed fails, we come back and make it right, free.

Our mission

Be the one number every homeowner in the River Valley keeps on the fridge.

That means being reachable, being honest about what a job costs, and doing work we would be glad to have in our own houses. Everything River Valley Handyman Co. does comes back to four habits.

  • Show up when we said

    A two-hour window, a text when we are ten minutes out, and a call the moment anything slips.

  • Quote it straight

    The number we give is the number on the invoice. If we find something extra, we stop and ask first.

  • Do it like it is ours

    Level, plumb, caulked and painted. We do not leave a job we would not want to look at every day.

  • Leave it cleaner

    Drop cloths, shop vac, old parts hauled off. The only evidence we were there is that it works now.
