Privacy

Short, because there isn't much to say.

Ship Notes collects an email address so it can send you an email. Everything below is detail on that one sentence.

What we store

Your email address, your first name if you gave one, the focus you picked on the form, and the date you subscribed. If you answered the optional "what would make this worth it" question, that text is kept so it can shape future issues; it is never published.

What we don't do

  • No open-tracking pixels. We do not know whether you read an issue.
  • No link tracking. Links go straight to where they say.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing the list. The classified ad at the bottom of each issue is sold on subscriber count, never on your address.
  • No analytics on this site. The host keeps standard server logs for 30 days.

The email service that sends the newsletter can see the list because it has to. That is the only third party, and it is bound by a contract that says it can't use the list for anything else.

Leaving and deleting

  1. Every issue has a one-click unsubscribe link. It works instantly.
  2. To delete everything, reply to any issue with "delete me" or email hello@example.com. Done within a week, by hand.

Last updated 2026. Changes get announced in the issue itself and posted here with the date.
