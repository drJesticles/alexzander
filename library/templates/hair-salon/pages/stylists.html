Stylists

Six chairs, six specialties.

Every stylist at Lumen Hair Studio trains at least twice a year. Pick the one whose work looks like what you want, or let the front desk match you.

Renee Castillo

Owner, master colorist

Eighteen years behind the chair, Redken certified educator. Balayage, lived-in blondes and the corrections other salons send over.

Jordan Pike

Precision cuts, short hair, grey blending

Trained at Vidal Sassoon Chicago. Bobs, pixies, men's scissor work and the kind of fringe that grows out well.

Maya Tran

Extensions and curly hair

Certified in hand-tied, tape-in and K-tip extensions. Also our curl specialist: cuts dry, styles wet, never flat-irons a curl without asking.

Chloe Bennett

Bridal, updos and blowouts

Runs our on-site wedding team. Has styled parties at Mount Nebo, Lake Dardanelle and most barns between here and Danville.

Marcus Hale

Men's cuts and fades

Barber-trained, salon-finished. Skin fades, beard shaping, hot towel shaves, and the Tech students who want something that photographs well.

Sofia Reyes

New talent stylist

Finished our associate program this spring. Cuts and single-process color at 20% off menu prices while she builds her book, supervised by Renee.
