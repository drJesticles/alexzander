Good to know

Before you book.

The questions we answer most at the front desk. Anything else, text us and a stylist replies between clients.

How far out do I need to book?
Cuts, about a week. Color, two to three weeks, and Saturdays fill a month ahead. Thursday evenings are the easiest slots to get.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancel or move up to 24 hours before for free. Inside 24 hours, half the service price. No-shows pay the full amount before rebooking.
Do I need a consultation first?
For a cut, no, it happens in the chair. For color correction, extensions or anything major, yes: a free 15-minute visit so we can see your hair and quote a real price.
Should I come with clean hair?
For cuts, either way. For color, a day or two of natural oil is fine and a little helpful. For extensions, please come washed and dry with no product.
Is the price on the menu what I will pay?
Almost always. Very long or very thick hair can add $20 to $40 because it takes more product and time. Your stylist tells you before starting, never after.
What products do you use?
Redken and Pulp Riot color, Oribe and Kevin Murphy styling, and K18 for repair. Everything on the shelf is what we use in the chair.
Can you come to my wedding venue?
Yes, for parties of four or more within an hour of Russellville. On-site styling starts at $125 per person plus a travel fee past 20 miles.
What if I do not love it?
Tell us within a week and we fix it at no charge. We would much rather adjust a cut than have you quietly go somewhere else.
