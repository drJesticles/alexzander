Privacy

What we do with your email address.

You give Signal Coffee Roasters an email address so we can tell you when we open. That is the whole deal. Here it is in slightly more detail.

What we collect

The email address you type into the form, the time you submitted it, and which form it came from (the launch notice or the monthly notes). Nothing else. No names, no phone numbers unless you call us.

How we use it

  • One email when the doors open, with the date, hours and the first-week menu.
  • If you also joined the monthly notes, one email a month until you leave.
  • An invitation to the soft-opening tasting if you're in the first hundred.

We don't sell, trade or share the list with anyone. Our email tool can see it because it has to send the mail; that's the only third party.

Leaving

  1. Click the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email. It works immediately.
  2. Or reply with "remove" and a human will do it by hand.
  3. Or email hello@example.com and ask us to delete everything we have, which is one row.

This site keeps standard server logs for 30 days and runs no analytics or ad trackers.
