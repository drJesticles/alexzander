Work history

Roles, most recent first.

  1. 2023–now

    Lead Front-End Engineer, Northstar Health (remote)

    Own the web platform for patient scheduling and billing across 12 hospitals. Manage five engineers. Led a migration from a legacy Angular app to React and TypeScript with zero downtime, cut median page load from 4.1s to 1.6s, and shipped an accessibility program that passed a third-party WCAG 2.2 AA audit.

  2. 2021–2023

    Senior Front-End Engineer, Northstar Health (remote)

    Rebuilt the appointment booking flow used by 40,000 patients a month. Introduced a component library with Storybook and visual regression tests. Paired weekly with clinical staff to cut the booking flow from nine screens to four.

  3. 2019–2021

    Front-End Engineer, Ledgerline (Austin, TX)

    Built the real-time dispatch dashboard for a freight logistics startup: live maps, WebSocket updates, offline-tolerant forms for drivers. Designed and documented the design system that the company still uses. Second engineer on a team that grew to nine.

  4. 2017–2019

    Web Developer, Parkway Digital (Little Rock, AR)

    Shipped 30-plus marketing and e-commerce sites for regional clients including a credit union, a hospital foundation and a restaurant group. Learned performance budgets, CMS theming and how to explain a bug to a client without jargon.

  5. 2013–2017

    B.S. Computer Science, Arkansas Tech University

    Minor in graphic design. Built the student housing portal as a senior project; it ran for four years after graduation. Worked summers doing IT for a Russellville school district.
