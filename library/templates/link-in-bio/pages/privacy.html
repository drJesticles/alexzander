Privacy

What this page knows about you (almost nothing).

This is a links page for @maya.makes. It exists to send you somewhere else. Here is what happens on the way.

On this site

No cookies, no analytics scripts, no pixels. The host keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested) for up to 30 days for security, and that is the whole list.

Where the links go

  • Shop links go to my store, which has its own privacy policy and handles payment.
  • Video and podcast links go to the platforms that host them. They track what they track.
  • The newsletter link goes to a sign-up form. One email a month; unsubscribe is one click.
  • The email link opens your mail app. What you send me stays in my inbox.

I don't sell, rent or share email addresses. Ever. If you write to me about a commission, that conversation is between us.

Questions

Email hello@example.com and I'll answer within a few days, usually with clay on my hands.
