Privacy policy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

The short version: Avant Movers collects what it needs to quote and complete your move, and nothing else. We don't sell your information and we don't send marketing you didn't ask for.

What we collect

When you request a quote or book a move, we collect your name, phone number, email, pickup and delivery addresses, and the details of what's moving. If you pay by card, the card details go straight to our payment processor and never touch our systems.

Our website records basic, anonymous visit statistics (pages viewed, rough location, device type) so we know what people are looking for. No advertising trackers are installed.

How we use it

  • To call or text you back about your quote and confirm your move date.
  • To dispatch the crew and get them to the right addresses.
  • To send your invoice and, once, a request for a review after the move.
  • To handle any damage claim, which may require sharing details with our insurer.

We will never share your addresses, phone number or move details with anyone who is not directly involved in moving your things.

How long we keep it

  1. Quote requests that don't become moves are deleted after 12 months.
  2. Completed move records are kept for seven years for tax and insurance purposes.
  3. You can ask us to delete anything not required by law at any time.
