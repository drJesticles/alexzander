A typical bathroom refresh

What the week looks like.

Every remodel gets a day-by-day plan before we start, so you know when the water is off and when it is back on.

  1. Day 0

    Walkthrough and measurements

    We confirm vanity, tile, glass and paint with you in the showroom, photograph everything, and lock the materials order.

  2. Day 1

    Demo and protection

    Floors and hallway covered, old vanity and tile out, dumpster hauled the same afternoon.

  3. Day 2–3

    Rough-in and tile

    Plumbing moved if needed, backer board set, tile laid and grouted. Water to that bathroom only is off on day two; the rest of the house runs normally.

  4. Day 4

    Vanity, fixtures, paint

    New vanity and faucet installed, exhaust fan swapped, two coats on the walls, trim caulked.

  5. Day 5

    Punch list and handoff

    You walk it with the lead tech, we fix anything on the list, then we clean and clear out before dinner.
