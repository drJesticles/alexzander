Three ways to do a kitchen

Refresh, reface or rebuild.

Refresh: new surfaces, same boxes

Keep the cabinets, replace everything you touch. Counters, sink, faucet, backsplash, hardware, lighting and paint in about a week. The biggest change for the smallest budget.

  • Quartz or butcher-block counters
  • Tile backsplash and new sink
  • Cabinet paint and new hardware
Price a refresh

Reface: new doors, drawers and finish

If the boxes are solid, we skin them, hang new doors and drawer fronts, add soft-close hardware and pull-outs, then do counters and backsplash. Looks new, costs about half.

  • New shaker or slab doors in 40 colors
  • Soft-close hinges and drawer slides
  • Pull-out trash, spice and pantry inserts
Price a reface

Rebuild: new layout, new everything

Take out a wall, add an island, move the sink to the window. New cabinets, counters, floors, lighting and plumbing. Permits, inspections and engineering handled by us.

  • Wall removal with engineered beams
  • Islands with seating and storage
  • Full plumbing and electrical relocation

Good to know

Quick answers.

The short version of what we get asked most. If yours is not here, call or text and a human answers.

What are your hours?
Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Showroom open Saturday mornings. Crews work weekdays only.
How long will I be without a kitchen?
Refresh: about five working days. Reface: eight to ten. Rebuild: three weeks. We set up a temporary sink and counter in the garage or dining room.
How do I pay?
Three stages: 30% at signing to order cabinets and counters, 40% after rough-in, 30% after your walkthrough. Card, check or bank transfer.
Do I need to be home?
Only for the first morning and the final walkthrough. Most customers leave a garage code. You get a photo update every evening.
Can I pick cabinets and counters myself?
Yes. The showroom has doors, counter slabs and tile to take home. If you have seen something online, bring the link and we will source it or match it.
Do you pull permits?
Yes, for anything structural, electrical or plumbing. The fee is a line on your proposal and we schedule the inspections.
Do you do appliances?
We install them and build around them. Buy from whoever you like; send us the spec sheets and we size the openings exactly.
Is the work guaranteed?
Workmanship is covered for two years. Cabinets and counters carry the manufacturer's warranty, and we handle the claim for you.
