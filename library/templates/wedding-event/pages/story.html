Our story

From a crawfish boil to a lakeside altar.

  1. 2020

    The crawfish boil

    A mutual friend's backyard in Russellville, May 2020. Mateo said Claire was peeling them wrong. Claire said he could show her. He could not.

  2. 2021

    First trip, first fight, first dog

    A weekend at Petit Jean that turned into a week when the car died. Came home with a shelter mutt named Biscuit and a decision to stop pretending this was casual.

  3. 2023

    The house on Parkway

    A 1962 brick ranch with a terrible kitchen and a perfect back porch. We fixed the kitchen. The porch is where most of the wedding was planned.

  4. 2025

    The question, on the water

    Lake Dardanelle, October, the same cove where we'll say our vows. Mateo had the ring in a tackle box for three weeks. Claire found it on week two and said nothing.

  5. 2027

    June 13

    Four o'clock, lakeside, with the people who got us here. Both dogs will be in attendance and neither will behave.
