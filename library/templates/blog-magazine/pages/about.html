From the editor

Why we started a magazine about the Ozarks.

Eureka Springs, Arkansas

Dear reader,

I spent eleven years at a daily paper in Little Rock watching the regional desk shrink from six people to one, and then to none. The last story I filed from the Ozarks was about a flood. Nobody had been sent for the thirty years of ordinary life before it.

The Ozark Ledger started in 2022 as an email to two hundred friends. It is now a small newsroom of five, a free Sunday letter to 18,000 people, and a quarterly print issue that goes to 1,400 paying members. We write about food, land, music and the people who make things, and we take as long as the story takes.

We do not run ads and we do not sell the list. Members pay for the whole thing, which means we answer to them and nobody else. If you ever think we have got something wrong, write to me at editor@ozarkledger.com. I read every one.

Nell Carver

Nell CarverEditor, The Ozark Ledger
