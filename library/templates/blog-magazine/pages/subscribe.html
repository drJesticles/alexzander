Membership questions

The practical bits.

Billing, print, gifts and the archive. Anything else, write to editor@ozarkledger.com.

Is the website paywalled?
No. Every story is free to read on the site two weeks after it goes to members. We would rather be read than be exclusive.
When does the print issue ship?
The first week of January, April, July and October. Join by the 15th of the previous month to get the next one; otherwise your subscription starts with the issue after.
Can I give a membership as a gift?
Yes, at the same prices. We send a card by post or email on the date you choose, and the recipient manages the account from then on.
Do you ship print outside the US?
To Canada for an extra $12 a year. Elsewhere, email us; postage is usually $25 and we will tell you exactly before you pay.
How do I cancel?
One link at the bottom of every email, or reply and ask. No phone call, no retention offer, no hard feelings.
Where does the money go?
Five salaries, mileage, print and postage, in that order. We publish the annual numbers in the January issue.
Can I pitch a story?
Please do. Two paragraphs to the editor, with why it is an Ozarks story and why you are the one to write it. We pay $600 to $1,500 for features and we answer every pitch.
Can I buy back issues?
While they last, $15 each at the general store in Eureka Springs or from the link in any Sunday letter. Issue one sold out and will not be reprinted.
