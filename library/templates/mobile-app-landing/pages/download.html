Before you download

Quick answers.

What people ask on the store page. Anything else, email help@pace.run and a human replies.

Is it really free?
The Couch to 5K plan and the voice coach are free, forever, no ads. The coaching plan ($6 a month or $48 a year) adds the 10K, maintenance and comeback plans, watch apps and progress history.
Which phones and watches?
iPhone running iOS 16 or later, Android 11 or later. Apple Watch Series 5 and up, Garmin watches with Connect IQ, and any Bluetooth heart-rate strap.
I have never run. Can I really do this?
Week one is sixty seconds of jogging at a time, eight times, with walking in between. If you can walk briskly for twenty minutes, you can do week one.
Treadmill or outside?
Either. The app counts time, not distance, so a treadmill works fine. Outdoors it maps your route if you let it, and you can turn that off.
What about my data?
Runs are stored on your phone and, if you choose, backed up to your account. We do not sell data, show ads or share location with anyone. Delete everything from Settings in one tap.
Can I use my own music or podcasts?
Yes. The coach's cues duck your audio for a few seconds and bring it back. Works with Spotify, Apple Music, Pocket Casts and anything else that plays audio.
How do I cancel?
From your phone's subscription settings, any time. The free plan keeps working and your run history stays on your device.
Who makes Pace?
A team of six in Boulder, Colorado, three of whom learned to run with the app before they worked on it. Support is answered by the same people.
