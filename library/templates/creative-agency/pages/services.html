Good to know

Quick answers.

The short version of what new clients ask. If yours is not here, email or book a call and a human answers.

Do you only work locally?
No. About half our clients are outside Arkansas. Design, web and marketing happen entirely over video call and shared links. Only physical installs need us on site.
How long does a website take?
A starter site launches in about three weeks from the day we get your content. Bigger builds run six to eight. We put the dates in the quote.
Who owns the site and the files?
You do, outright. Domain in your name, every logo file in your inbox, and the site editable by you without us.
How do I pay?
Half to start, half at launch, by card or bank transfer. Monthly plans bill on the first and cancel any time with 30 days' notice.
Can you write the words too?
Yes. Most clients hand us a rough draft or a recorded call and we write the rest. Copywriting is included in every website quote.
What if I already have a logo?
We will work with it. If it needs cleanup for the web or embroidery, we tidy the files for a flat $250.
What about the smart-home side?
Same company, same people. Our tech wing installs cameras, locks, thermostats and displays locally, and sets up gear remotely for clients anywhere.
Is there a guarantee?
If a site we built breaks because of our work in the first year, we fix it free. Marketing plans show real numbers monthly; if they are not moving, we say so.
