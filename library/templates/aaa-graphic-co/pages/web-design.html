Good to know

Quick answers about website projects.

The short version of what we get asked most. If yours is not here, email or book a call and a human answers.

Do we have to meet in person?
No. Every project runs on video calls and a shared folder. Most clients are outside Arkansas; the Russellville ones still prefer Zoom.
Who writes the words?
We do, with you. The kickoff call gives us the raw material; we draft, you correct. Nobody expects you to write a homepage.
Can I edit it myself afterward?
Yes. Text, photos, hours and prices are all editable without touching code. You get a recorded training session and written notes.
What platform do you build on?
Whatever fits the job. Most small business sites go on a managed builder for speed and easy editing; stores and booking sites get the platform their tools need.
How do I pay?
Half at kickoff, half at launch. Card or bank transfer. Growth projects can be split into three.
Do you do hosting and maintenance?
Hosting is yours, billed at cost. Ongoing updates are available on a monthly retainer on the marketing page, or hourly when you need a hand.
What if I already have a logo?
We use it. If it needs a clean-up to work on screen, we'll say so and quote it separately. We never force a rebrand.
How long does it really take?
Launch sites: two weeks. Business sites: four. Growth: six to ten. The clock usually waits on content from your side, so we help you gather it early.
