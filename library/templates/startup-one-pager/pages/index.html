Autonomous weeding for row crops · Ames, Iowa

Weeds pulled. Herbicide skipped. Fields walked by robots, not crews.

Kestrel Robotics builds a solar-charged weeding robot that covers 40 acres a day in soybeans, cotton and vegetables, identifying every plant with a camera and removing weeds mechanically. No chemicals, no labour shortage, no 3am phone calls.

Request a 2027 pilot How it works
The product

One robot. Three jobs a farmer currently pays for separately.

01

Sees every plant, fifty times a second

Six downward cameras and an on-board model trained on 40 million labelled images tell crop from weed at 2 cm resolution. Accuracy in last season's trials: 98.4 percent on soybeans, 97.1 percent on cotton.

Ask for the trial data
02

Pulls the weed, not the crop

Twelve independent arms remove weeds mechanically at up to 2,400 per minute. No herbicide, no resistance problem, and the robot works in fields already certified organic.

See it in the field
03

Maps the field while it works

Every pass produces a plant-level map: weed pressure, stand counts, emergence gaps. Farmers get it on their phone that evening. Agronomists get an API.
