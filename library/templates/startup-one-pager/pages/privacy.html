Privacy policy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

This page covers the Kestrel Robotics website, the pilot programme and the farm dashboard. It is written to be read, not skimmed. Last updated October 2026.

On this website

When you send the contact form we store your name, email, phone if you gave it, and your message, so we can reply. We use a privacy-respecting analytics tool that records page views without cookies or personal identifiers. We do not run advertising pixels.

In the pilot programme

  • Field boundaries, crop type and planting dates you give us, so the robot knows where to work.
  • Images captured by the robot's cameras, which contain plants and soil. Faces are not captured by design: the cameras point down.
  • Robot telemetry: position, battery, arm counts and faults, used for support and to improve the product.

Field data belongs to the farm. We use it to run your robot and to train models that benefit every farm on the platform. We do not sell it, share it with input suppliers, or disclose yields to anyone.

If a buyer, lender or insurer asks us for your field data, the answer is no unless you tell us otherwise in writing.

Your choices

  1. Download a complete copy of your farm's data from the dashboard at any time.
  2. Ask us to delete it when a pilot ends. Robot imagery already used to train a model cannot be separated out, but the field it came from is not identifiable.
  3. Opt out of product-improvement telemetry; support telemetry is required while a robot is in your field.

Questions go to hello@kestrelrobotics.com or by post to 2321 North Loop Drive, Ames, IA 50010.

We will email every pilot farm and dashboard user if this policy changes in a way that affects them, at least 30 days before it takes effect.

The specifics, by topic.

Retention, security, subprocessors and your legal rights. Expand any section.

How long we keep data

Contact form messages: two years. Pilot field data and dashboard history: for the life of the agreement plus one year, unless you ask for deletion sooner. Robot telemetry: three years, after which it is aggregated and the raw records are deleted.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest in US data centres. Robots authenticate with per-unit hardware keys and receive signed firmware only. Access to farm data inside the company is limited to the support and field teams and is logged. We have never had a breach; if we do, affected farms are notified within 72 hours.

Who else touches the data

A cloud hosting provider, an email delivery service for dashboard notifications, and a cellular carrier for robot connectivity. Each is bound by a data-processing agreement. The current list is available on request and we tell dashboard users before adding one.

Your legal rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export personal data, and to object to certain uses. We honour these for everyone, not just where the law requires. Email the address above; we respond within 30 days and never charge for it.

Questions about any of this?

A person at Kestrel Robotics reads every message, including the ones about privacy.
