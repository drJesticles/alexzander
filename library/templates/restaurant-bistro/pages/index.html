Our story

A 1912 freight depot, a wood oven, and a 40-seat dining room.

The Depot Bistro opened in 2014 when chef Marisol Vance came home from twelve years in New Orleans kitchens and bought the empty Missouri Pacific depot on B Street. The brick is original, the oven was built by hand, and the menu changes every Tuesday based on what came in from farms in Atkins, Dover and Pottsville.

  • Sourced within 60 miles. Beef from Danville, eggs from Hector, greens from the Atkins co-op, trout from the Little Red.
  • Bread and pasta made daily. Sourdough goes in the oven at 4pm so it is still warm at the first seating.
  • Wine by the glass, fairly priced. Forty bottles, most under $50, half of them from small producers.
