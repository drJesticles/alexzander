About the store

A general store for things made nearby.

Hollow Creek Mercantile opened in 2019 in a former hardware store on Central Avenue in Bentonville. The idea was simple: one shop where the candle maker from Eureka Springs, the beekeeper from Pea Ridge and the woodworker from Siloam Springs could all sell under one roof, and where the people buying could meet them at the Saturday market out front.

  • Forty-one makers, all within an hour's drive, all paid within seven days of a sale.
  • Nothing from a catalogue. If it is on the shelf, we have been to the workshop.
  • Open seven days, with free pickup for online orders at the front counter.

How we buy

Four rules for what goes on the shelf.

Every item at Hollow Creek Mercantile passes the same four tests before it gets a price tag. Most of what we are offered does not.

  • Made within an hour

    Every maker is close enough that we can drive out, see the workshop and shake a hand. Most of them we have.

  • Fair to the maker

    We buy outright, not on consignment, and pay within seven days. Nobody waits for their money while it sits on our shelf.

  • We use it first

    Every candle gets burned, every jar opened, every board cut on. If it does not hold up in our kitchens, it does not go in yours.

  • Priced to be bought

    Handmade should not mean untouchable. Most items are under $50, and the gift box builder starts at $35.
