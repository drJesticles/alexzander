Before you place the order

Shipping, pickup and returns.

The short version. Anything else, call (479) 555-0121 during store hours.

When will it ship?
Orders placed before 2pm Central ship the same business day by USPS Ground. Most of Arkansas, Missouri and Oklahoma sees it in two days; the coasts in four or five.
How does pickup work?
Choose pickup at checkout and we text you when it is bagged, usually within the hour during store hours. Bring the order number or just tell us your name.
Can I return something?
Yes, within 30 days, in store or by mail. Unopened pantry items, unburned candles and unworn apparel get a full refund. Opened food cannot be returned, but tell us if something was off and we will make it right.
Do you gift wrap?
Free on any order with three or more items: kraft box, tissue and a handwritten card. Add a note in the order comments and we will write it out.
Is my card information safe?
Card details are entered into a field hosted by our payment processor and never touch our servers. We see the last four digits and nothing more.
Do you ship candles in summer?
Yes, in insulated packaging from June through September. If a candle arrives warped or sweating, send a photo and we replace it.

Why shop with us

  • Free shipping over $60 Flat $7 under that. Ships in one business day.
  • 30-day returns Bring it back or mail it back. No restocking fee.
  • Secure checkout Cards handled by our payment processor, never stored here.
  • Free local pickup Downtown Bentonville, usually ready the same day.
