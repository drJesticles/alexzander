Get involved

Four ways to help, from two hours to a standing shift.

Sort and stock

Monday and Wednesday mornings. Sort produce, check dates, stock the shelves. Two-hour shifts, no experience needed, ages 12 and up.

Sign up

Ride the truck

Mobile pantry days, every other Tuesday and Thursday. Load at 7am, drive to one town, hand out food, back by 1pm. Drivers need a clean license.

Sign up

Pack backpacks

Thursday nights, 6pm to 7:30pm. An assembly line, 600 bags, and pizza. Great for families, youth groups and office teams.

Sign up

Run a food drive

We supply the bins, the flyer and the pickup. Peanut butter, canned protein and cereal are always short. Schools, churches and offices welcome.

Start one

Want to volunteer? Leave a number.

Our volunteer coordinator at River Valley Food Project calls back within a day, finds a shift that fits your week, and gets you on the schedule. First shift is always with a buddy.

Open roles

Two paid positions and a board seat.

River Valley Food Project runs on four part-time staff and a volunteer board. As the truck route grows in Russellville and beyond, we need two more people on payroll.

  • Flexible hours, mostly mornings
  • Mileage reimbursed on the route
  • Work that you can see the point of

Mobile pantry driver and coordinator

Part time, 25 hrs · $19/hr · Clean license, can lift 50 lbs

Drive the refrigerated truck on the seven-town route, lead the volunteer crew at each stop, and keep the cold-chain log.

Volunteer and donor coordinator

Part time, 20 hrs · $18/hr · Spreadsheets and people

Schedule 600 volunteers, thank 1,200 donors, and run the Thursday packing night. Some evenings.

Email your resume

Send it to hello@rivervalleyfoodproject.org. Board inquiries welcome at the same address.

Every dollar given to River Valley Food Project buys about nine meals through our wholesale and gleaning partners. Monthly gifts keep the trucks running in July, when donations drop and need does not.
