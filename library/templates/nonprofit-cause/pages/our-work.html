Our work

Four programs, one goal: nobody in the River Valley skips a meal.

01

The community pantry

A grocery-style pantry on South Arkansas Avenue where guests push a cart and choose their own food: fresh produce, dairy, meat, and shelf-stable staples. Open Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. About 180 households a week. No ID, no income proof, no limit on visits.

Pantry hours and location
02

The mobile pantry

A refrigerated box truck that visits Dover, Hector, London, Atkins, Pottsville, Dardanelle and Danville every other week, usually at a fire station or a church lot. Drive up, pop the trunk, and volunteers load 40 to 60 pounds of groceries. For families with no car, we deliver.

See the route
03

Weekend backpacks and senior boxes

Every Friday, 600 kids at 14 schools in Pope and Yell counties take home a bag with six kid-friendly meals and snacks, packed Thursday night by volunteers. Once a month, 340 seniors who cannot get to a pantry get a 30-pound box delivered to the door with a knock and a conversation.
