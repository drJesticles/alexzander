Donor questions

Where the money goes.

Honest answers to what people ask before they give. Our audited financials and IRS Form 990 are on the contact page.

Is my gift tax-deductible?
Yes. We are a 501(c)(3). You get an emailed receipt immediately and a year-end summary every January.
How much goes to overhead?
Eight percent last year: four part-time salaries, insurance, and the truck. Ninety-two cents of every dollar bought and moved food.
Why money instead of cans?
We buy from the regional food bank at about 11 cents a pound. Your $25 buys more there than $25 at the grocery store. But we gladly take cans too.
Can I pick what my gift funds?
Yes. Note "backpacks," "truck" or "seniors" with your gift and it goes there. Unrestricted gifts go wherever the shelf is emptiest.
Does my employer match?
Many River Valley employers do, including the plants and the hospital. Email us the form and we fill out our side the same week.
Can I donate food directly?
Drop it at the loading dock Tue & Thu 9am–1pm · Sat 8am–11am. Peanut butter, canned meat, cereal and diapers are the most needed.
Will you share or sell my information?
Never. We do not rent our list, we do not send paper mail unless you ask, and you can unsubscribe from the quarterly email in one click.
How do I give stock or from an IRA?
Email hello@rivervalleyfoodproject.org and our treasurer sends the brokerage instructions. Qualified charitable distributions from an IRA are welcome.
