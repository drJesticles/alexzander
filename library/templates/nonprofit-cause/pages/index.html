Food bank & mobile pantry · Russellville, AR

No one in the River Valley should go hungry.

River Valley Food Project moved 1.2 million pounds of food last year through a pantry on Arkansas Avenue, a refrigerated truck that visits seven towns, and backpacks that go home with 600 kids every Friday.
