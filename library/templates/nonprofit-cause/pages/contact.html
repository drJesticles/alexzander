Find the pantry

The old feed store on South Arkansas.

Half a mile south of the railroad tracks, green metal building with the truck out front. Guests park in the gravel lot; donations go to the dock on the north side. The mobile pantry route is posted on the door and on our Facebook page.

$25 feeds a family of four for a week.

Every dollar given to River Valley Food Project buys about nine meals through our wholesale and gleaning partners. Monthly gifts keep the trucks running in July, when donations drop and need does not.
