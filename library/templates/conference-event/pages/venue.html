Venue

Arkansas Tech University.

Keynotes and Track A in Witherspoon Hall, Track B and workshops in the Tech building next door, lunch on the quad between them. Everything is within a two-minute walk, and River Valley Dev Conf volunteers in orange shirts will point the way.

Getting here and staying

Drive, fly, sleep.

Driving & parking

Russellville is on I-40, 75 minutes from Little Rock and 90 from Fort Smith or Fayetteville. Free parking in the Tech lot off North Arkansas Avenue both days.

Lot T-3, free

Flying in

Fly into Little Rock (LIT) or Northwest Arkansas (XNA). A conference shuttle runs from LIT Thursday evening and back Saturday night, $25 each way.
