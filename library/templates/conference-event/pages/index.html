Why come

Small conference. Big on substance.

Six things that make River Valley Dev Conf worth two days and a drive to Russellville.

24 talks, two tracks

Every session is 30 minutes with code on screen. Speakers are practitioners, selected blind from 180 proposals.

Six hands-on workshops

Half-day sessions on Friday afternoon, 25 seats each. Bring a laptop, leave with something running.

400 people, not 4,000

Small enough that you'll talk to the speakers at lunch. Hallway track is the point, not a side effect.

Priced for people, not budgets

Early-bird tickets from $149, student passes $39, and a sponsor-funded scholarship pool for anyone who asks.

Recorded, released free

Every talk is captioned and published within 30 days. Miss a track, catch it later, share it with your team.

Run by volunteers

Organized by the Russellville developer meetup since 2022. Surplus funds student tickets the following year.

Early-bird pricing ends January 31.

Save $100 on a two-day pass. Group rates for teams of five or more.
