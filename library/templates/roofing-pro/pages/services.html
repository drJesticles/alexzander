How every roof goes

Local crew. Written scope. Clean yard.

Six things Summit Ridge Roofing does on every job, from a $350 pipe-boot repair to a full tear-off.

Free, photographed inspections

We walk every slope and send you a photo report the same day, so you see exactly what we see before you decide anything.

Line-item quotes

Tear-off, decking, underlayment, shingles, flashing, disposal, each on its own line. Rotten decking is priced per sheet up front, not discovered later.

Licensed and insured

Arkansas contractor license, $2M general liability and workers' comp on every roofer. Ask for the certificate; we'll email it.

Our crew, not a sub

The foreman who inspects your roof runs the crew that installs it. No subcontractor roulette, no re-explaining what you wanted.

Nail-free yard guarantee

Tarps over the landscaping, a trailer under the eave, and three magnet sweeps before we leave. Find a nail, we come back.

10-year workmanship warranty

On top of the manufacturer's shingle warranty. If our work leaks inside ten years, we fix it and the damage, no charge.
