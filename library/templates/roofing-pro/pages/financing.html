The fine print, in plain English

Warranties, payment and claims, without the legalese.

Everything Summit Ridge Roofing promises, and what we ask of you in return. Expand any section.

Estimates and change orders

Every roof gets a line-item written quote after a free inspection. Rotten decking is priced per sheet up front. The number does not change unless you add work or we find something that was physically impossible to see, and then we stop and show you before continuing.

If we open a wall and find something we could not have seen, we stop, show you, and write a change order before continuing.

Scheduling and weather

You get an install date in writing and a call the day before when materials land. If rain is forecast we move the date rather than start a tear-off we cannot dry in; a roof is never left open overnight. Cancel any time before materials are delivered at no charge.

Warranty

Workmanship is guaranteed for ten years on replacements and two years on repairs. If our work leaks, we fix the roof and the interior damage it caused, free. Manufacturer shingle and metal warranties are registered in your name and handed over with the paperwork.

The warranty does not cover new storm damage, foot traffic from other trades, or work done on top of ours by someone else.

Payment

Repairs are due on completion. Replacements are 30 percent when materials are delivered and the balance after your walkthrough. On insurance jobs you pay only your deductible; we bill the carrier directly for the rest, including any supplement.
