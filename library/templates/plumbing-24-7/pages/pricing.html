Good to know

Quick answers.

The short version of what we get asked most. If yours is not here, call or text and a human answers.

What are your hours?
Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm. The emergency line is answered by a plumber 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included.
Is the service call fee extra?
No. The $75 daytime or $150 after-hours call fee is waived when you go ahead with the repair. You only pay it if you decide not to proceed.
How do I pay?
Card on the plumber's tablet, check or cash when the job is done. Sewer and water line replacements can be split 50/50 before and after.
Do I need to be home?
For an emergency, yes, someone needs to let us in. For scheduled work, many customers leave a garage code and we text photos when we arrive and leave.
Can you work on a rental I own?
Yes. We handle after-hours calls for several local property managers, coordinate with the tenant directly and send you the itemized invoice.
Do you pull permits?
Yes, for water heaters, gas lines and sewer work where the City of Russellville requires one. The fee is on your quote and we schedule the inspection.
What do you not do?
Septic tank pumping, well pumps and irrigation systems. We will give you the name of someone good for those.
Is the work guaranteed?
Labor is covered for two years and our fittings for life. Water heaters carry the manufacturer's warranty, and we handle the claim for you.
