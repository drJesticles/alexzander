Our history

Eighteen years in the River Valley.

  1. 2008

    One van, one license

    Dale passes the Arkansas master exam and starts Current Electric Co. out of his garage. First job: a 100-amp panel on a farmhouse in Hector.

  2. 2012

    First hire, first shop

    Luis joins as an apprentice and the company moves into a rented bay off Highway 7 with room for wire spools and a second van.

  3. 2016

    Commercial crew

    A restaurant build-out on Main Street leads to a second, then a clinic. A dedicated commercial crew now runs nights and weekends from Fort Smith to Little Rock.

  4. 2021

    EV chargers and generators

    Evan comes on as the first electric trucks show up in River Valley driveways. Chargers and standby generators are now a third of residential work.

  5. 2026

    Eight electricians, 4,000 jobs

    Same owner, same license on the wall. Most new work still comes from a neighbor who saw the van in a driveway.

Our mission

Be the electrician the inspector is glad to see.

That means pulling the permit, labeling the panel, and never leaving a splice outside a box. Everything Current Electric Co. does comes back to four habits.

  • Show up when we said

    A two-hour window, a text when we are ten minutes out, and a call the moment anything slips.

  • Quote it straight

    The number we give is the number on the invoice. If we find something extra, we stop and ask first.

  • Code, not "good enough"

    Every job is done to the current NEC whether or not an inspector will see it. The ones that get inspected pass the first time.

  • Label everything

    Every breaker named, every junction box accessible, every drawing left with the owner. The next electrician should thank us.
