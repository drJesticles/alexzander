Before the visit

Three small things that make the day go smoothly.

None of these are required. All of them help the AAA Home Specialists tech get straight to work.

  1. Clear the work area. A couple of feet around the faucet, fan or wall section. We'll move furniture if needed, but it costs you time on the clock.
  2. Put pets somewhere calm. Open doors and power tools are a bad combination for a curious dog.
  3. Have the parts out, if you bought them. If not, we bring the common stuff and bill it at cost.

On the day

You'll get a text when the tech leaves the previous job, usually ten minutes out. They'll walk through the plan with you before the first screw comes out, and walk the finished work with you before they leave. The invoice arrives that evening with a pay link; card, check or bank transfer all work.

Labor is guaranteed for one year. If anything we did comes loose or leaks, we come back and make it right at no charge.

Running late yourself? A quick text is all we need; we'll shuffle the route rather than knock on an empty house.
