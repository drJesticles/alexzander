About

Trained in Basel, based in Portland, working anywhere with a decent connection.

Elin spent six years in two large agencies before leaving in 2019 to work directly with the people who make decisions. The studio is one designer, a rotating cast of trusted collaborators for type, motion and code, and no account managers.

Elin Marren takes on four to six projects a year. Each gets full attention from the brief to the handover document, and most clients come back for the next thing.

Background

Thirteen years, five chapters.

  1. 2013

    Basel School of Design

    Two years of grids, letterforms and being told to take things out. Still the most useful instruction ever received.

  2. 2015

    Agency years, Zurich and New York

    Identity work for banks, a transit authority and two museums. Learned how large systems are built and why most of them fail.

  3. 2019

    Independent

    Opened the studio in Portland with one client and a borrowed desk. First project: a wayfinding system for a regional hospital.

  4. 2022

    Marren Grotesk

    Released a self-initiated typeface in two weights. Now used on three client projects and a city's parking signs.

  5. 2026

    Thirty-eight projects on

    Same desk, better chair. Teaching one studio course a year at the Pacific Northwest College of Art.
