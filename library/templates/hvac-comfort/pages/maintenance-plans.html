The 21-point tune-up

What actually happens at a tune-up.

About an hour per system, spring for cooling and fall for heating. Also available without a plan for a flat $99.

Book a tune-up

What we test and measure

  • Refrigerant pressures and superheat/subcooling
  • Capacitor, contactor and compressor amp draw
  • Heat exchanger and carbon monoxide at the registers
  • Blower motor, belt and static pressure
  • Thermostat calibration and wiring
  • Ductwork for leaks, insulation and airflow at every register

What we clean and replace

  • Replace the filter (included on Comfort and Total)
  • Flush the condensate drain and treat the pan
  • Wash the outdoor coil and straighten fins
  • Clean the flame sensor and igniter
  • Tighten electrical connections and lubricate motors
  • Leave a written report with photos, readings and a system age estimate

Anything we find that needs real repair gets a separate flat-rate quote with your plan discount applied. You decide; there is never pressure to do it that day.

Good to know

Quick answers.

The short version of what we get asked most. If yours is not here, call or text and a human answers.

What are your hours?
Monday to Friday, 7am to 6pm. No-heat and no-cool calls answered seven days, around the clock.
Can I cancel a plan?
Any time after your first tune-up. No contract, no cancellation fee. If you move within the River Valley, the plan moves with you.
How do I pay?
Plans are billed monthly to a card on file. Repairs are due on completion, card or check. New systems can be financed from $89 a month.
Do I need to be home for a tune-up?
Only if the air handler is inside. Many customers leave a garage or gate code. We text photos and the report when we are done.
Can I put a rental on a plan?
Yes. We schedule with the tenant, bill you, and the second system on any property is half price. Several local property managers have every unit on Comfort.
Do you pull permits?
Yes, on every system replacement, and we schedule the inspection. Repairs and tune-ups do not need one.
What do you not do?
Window units, boilers and commercial refrigeration. We will give you the name of someone good for those.
Is the work guaranteed?
Repairs are covered for two years, parts and labor. New systems carry the manufacturer's parts warranty, extended to ten years parts and labor on the Total plan.
