About us

Family-owned, and still answering our own phone.

Comfort Zone Heating & Air started in 2006 with one van and one promise: show up when the house is 90 degrees, fix what is actually broken, and never sell a system that is not needed. Twenty years on, we are still based in Russellville, still answering our own phone, and still fixing most systems the same day we are called.

  • Licensed and certified. Arkansas HVACR license, NATE and EPA 608 on every tech, $2M liability.
  • Priced before we start. Flat-rate repairs from a printed book, written estimates on new systems, no surprise line items.
  • Guaranteed for two years. If a repair fails, we come back and make it right, parts and labor, free.

Our mission

Be the number every homeowner in the River Valley keeps on the thermostat.

That means being reachable on the worst day of summer, being honest about repair versus replace, and installing systems we would put in our own houses. Everything Comfort Zone Heating & Air does comes back to four habits.

  • Show up when we said

    A two-hour window, a text when we are ten minutes out, and a call the moment anything slips.

  • Repair before replace

    If a part fixes it, we fix it. We show you the readings, both numbers, and let you decide. No commission on sales.

  • Size it right

    Every new system gets a real load calculation. Oversized units short-cycle and sweat; undersized ones never catch up. Neither is cheaper.

  • Leave it cleaner

    Drop cloths, shop vac, old parts hauled off. The only evidence we were there is that it works now.
