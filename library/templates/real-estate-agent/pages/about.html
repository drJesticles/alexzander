Hadley Pike sold her first house in 2012. It was her grandmother's.

She grew up on a cattle farm outside Atkins, worked eight years in the Pope County assessor's office, and got her license when it became clear nobody selling her grandmother's farmhouse understood what it was actually worth. Pike Realty Group opened the office on Arkansas Avenue in 2016.

Our history

Fourteen years, six hundred closings.

  1. 2012

    The farmhouse

    Hadley gets licensed to sell her grandmother's farmhouse outside Atkins after two agents undervalue it. It sells in nine days for $22,000 more than either suggested.

  2. 2016

    The office on Arkansas Avenue

    Pike Realty Group opens downtown with Hadley and Jolene. First year: 38 closings, most of them referrals from the farmhouse neighbors.

  3. 2019

    Drone and 3D on every listing

    We become the first office in the county to put a 3D walkthrough on every listing, not just the expensive ones. Days on market drops from 34 to 21.

  4. 2022

    Marcus and Dale join

    Relocation and land become their own specialties as the plant expansion brings families from Texas and Oklahoma looking for acreage.

  5. 2026

    600th closing

    Still one office, still four agents on purpose. Seventy-one percent of this year's clients came from a past client's recommendation.
