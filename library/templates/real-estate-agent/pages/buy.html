Buying with Pike Realty Group

From first call to keys, usually in 45 days.

Buyers never pay us directly; our fee comes from the sale. Here is what the six weeks look like for most of our clients.

  1. Week 1

    Coffee and a pre-approval

    Thirty minutes at the office or on the phone. We learn what you need, and connect you with two local lenders who can pre-approve you in a day. That letter is what makes sellers take your offer seriously.

  2. Week 1–3

    Showings, including the ones not online yet

    You get alerts the hour something lists. We tour on evenings and weekends, and we tell you the things the listing will not: the drainage, the road noise, the roof age.

  3. Week 3

    The offer

    We pull the last 90 days of sales to price it, write the contract, and negotiate. Our buyers paid an average of 2.1% under asking last year.

  4. Week 4–5

    Inspection and appraisal

    We schedule the inspector, attend the inspection with you, and negotiate repairs or credits. The lender orders the appraisal; we handle it if it comes in low.

  5. Week 6

    Final walkthrough and closing

    We walk the house the morning of closing, you sign at the title company on Main Street, and you get the keys before lunch.

Buyer questions

What first-time buyers ask us.

Less than most people think. FHA loans start at 3.5% down, USDA loans for most of Pope and Yell counties outside Russellville city limits are zero down, and VA loans are zero down for veterans. On a $250,000 house, 3.5% is $8,750.

Typically 2% to 3% of the price for a buyer in Arkansas: lender fees, title insurance, appraisal, a year of homeowner's insurance and prepaid taxes. We often negotiate for the seller to cover some or all of it.

In nearly every sale in this market, the seller pays our fee from the proceeds. We put our agreement in writing before the first showing so there are no surprises, and if a seller will not cover it we tell you before you make an offer.

Well-priced homes under $300,000 in Russellville still get multiple offers within a week. Above that, and in the outlying towns, buyers have more room to negotiate. We will tell you which situation your target house is in before you offer.
