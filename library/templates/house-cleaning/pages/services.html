The checklist

What a standard clean includes, and what a deep clean adds.

Every team works from the same printed checklist and leaves it on the counter with each item ticked. If something was skipped, you will know why.

Book a clean

Every standard clean

  • Kitchen: counters, sink, stovetop, outside of appliances, floors
  • Bathrooms: toilets, tubs, showers, mirrors, floors
  • Dusting every surface, shelf and sill within reach
  • Vacuum and mop every floor, under furniture where it moves
  • Beds made, trash out, fresh liners
  • Door handles, switches and remotes wiped

Deep clean adds

  • Inside the oven, fridge and microwave
  • Baseboards, door frames and ceiling fans
  • Blinds, light fixtures and vent covers
  • Tile grout scrubbed, shower glass de-scaled
  • Cabinet fronts and walls spot-washed
  • Inside windows and window tracks

Most recurring customers start with a deep clean so the standard visits only have to maintain. Add-ons like inside the fridge can be booked on any visit.
