Good to know

Quick answers about pricing.

The short version of what we get asked most. If yours is not here, call or text and a human answers.

What are your hours?
Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. We do not clean on Sundays.
How do you price bigger homes?
Add $20 per bedroom or bathroom over 3 and 2. Finished basements, bonus rooms and home offices count as bedrooms. Homes over 3,500 square feet get a quote from photos.
How do I pay?
A card on file is charged after each clean, never before. You get an emailed receipt with the checklist and photos.
Do I need to be home?
No. Most customers leave a door or garage code. We text when we arrive and leave, and lock up behind us.
Do you charge extra for pets?
No. We love them. If shedding is heavy we may suggest weekly instead of every other week, but the price per visit is the same.
What if I need to skip a week?
Text us by 5pm the day before and there is no charge. Same-day cancellations are $50 so the team is not left without work.
What do you not do?
Carpet shampooing, exterior windows, mold and biohazard. We will give you the name of someone good for those.
Is the clean guaranteed?
Yes. If anything on the checklist was missed, text a photo within 24 hours and the team comes back to fix it free.
