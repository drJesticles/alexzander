Billing and account

The admin questions.

Plans, invoices, seats and cancelling. For anything about your specific account, email support@beacon.dev from the address on the account.

Is there a free plan?
Yes: 10 monitors at a 3-minute interval, one status page, email and Slack alerts. No card, no expiry.
How do seats work?
Team and Business are priced per account, not per user. Invite as many people as you like; only monitors and status pages are metered.
Can I get an invoice instead of paying by card?
On yearly Business plans, yes. Net-30 invoicing and purchase orders are set up by support in about a day.
What if I cancel mid-year?
Yearly plans are refunded pro rata for unused whole months. Monthly plans run to the end of the period. Data stays exportable for 90 days.
Do you have a security questionnaire ready?
Yes. SOC 2 Type II report, pen-test summary and a completed CAIQ are available under Settings, then Security, on the Business plan, or by request.
Can I transfer the account to someone else?
Any owner can make another member an owner and then leave. If the only owner has left the company, support can reassign it after verifying with your domain's admin.
Where is data stored?
US data centres by default; EU residency is available on Business. Check results are encrypted at rest and in transit.
Do you offer discounts?
Open-source projects and registered nonprofits get Team free. Yearly billing is two months off on every plan.

Can't find it? A person can.

Support at Beacon is staffed by engineers, weekdays 6am to 6pm Pacific. Median first reply is nine minutes.
